# IP, branding and asset policy

## Game identity
Name is a working title and requires basic conflict/availability check before logo/domain registration. Final brand should be original.

## GTA VI relationship
GTA VI releasing November 19 2026 is relevant to audience attention, but **Escape 60 is not affiliated with, endorsed by or based on assets from Rockstar/Take-Two**. Do not copy GTA logos, Vice City map, audio, characters, UI trade dress, loading art or proprietary car models. Original police-chase arcade mechanics are a genre, not a license to use others' content.
