# Market research — Iran, 2026-11-19

## Verified signals
1. **Mobile casual scale**: Namafar cites Cafe Bazaar as of 11 Aban 1405; word game Amirza at ~30M installed devices; driving and arcade titles also feature strongly. Installed devices are **not** daily active players or browser click conversion.
   - https://namafar.com/en/analysis/200076/most-installed-mobile-games-on-iran-s-cafe-bazaar-in-aban-1405-2026-27
2. **Streamer attention**: Iranian game streamer reporting from 23 Aban 1405 identifies Minecraft, Call of Duty, GTA and Roblox as major subjects; data primarily follows **Aparat**, not all platforms.
   - https://www.mehrnews.com/ (find 23 Aban 1405 streaming report), secondary: https://hamsafarbato.com/culture-and-history/2026/64933/
   - DIREC posts 29.6% Minecraft streamer share (not market share): https://www.linkedin.com/company/digital-games-research-center-direc-/
3. **GTA VI release timing**: Rockstar's official site states November 19 2026 release; this news creates short-term attention, **not proof** of interest in our browser game.
   - https://www.rockstargames.com/VI
   - https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026
4. **Competition**: official organizer announcement requires deployment on Edge and game link by 9 Azar 1405.
   - https://t.me/s/arvancloud

## Why Escape 60
Overlap between accessible driving fantasy + fast arcade loop + strong short-video/challenge format. It's a **hypothesis** about conversion/replay, not a statistically proven winning concept.

## Alternatives retained
- AI Duel: adaptive 60s brain/memory pattern puzzles. Easier content generation, less motor thrill.
- Impossible Parkour: Roblox-inspired escalating platform challenge; higher complexity depending on camera.
- Horror Doors: dramatic streamer moments, more demanding sound/atmosphere.
- Word Battle: broad Iranian casual audience; easier execution, lower natural driving tie-in.
- Mad Driver: simple endless traffic dodging; fastest build but weak differentiation without a twist.

## Strategic risk
- Giant GTA VI launch may compete for attention rather than funnel players to a new small game.
- Browser audience may differ from Android installed-app audience.
- A streamer view ≠ unique player in this competition.
- GTA IP must not be copied; we use only genre conventions and independently designed assets.

## Research validation plan
A/B test 2 landing hooks; 5–10-person usability test; actual unique first plays, retention, replays and shared-link completion after publishing.
