# Ethical growth playbook

## Primary sharing mechanism
At end of run, create a share card and canonical link `https://<game-host>/c/<challenge-id>` to let a friend play **same challenge rules/seed**. Show the player's actual run only if validated; unvalidated sample must say sample.

## Campaign channels
- Relevant short-form creator tests: racing/challenge gaming creators with transparent outreach.
- Instagram Stories and Telegram groups with opt-in shares.
- Community leaderboard daily recap; no fabricated ranks or percentiles.

## Launch hooks (copy candidates)
- «۶۰ ثانیه از پلیس فرار کن. رکورد من رو می‌زنی؟»
- «تا ثانیه ۴۷ رفتم؛ تو چند ثانیه دووم میاری؟»
- “60 seconds. One chase. Beat my time.”

## Growth ethics
No bots, purchased fake traffic, forced sharing, fabricated reviews, spam, undisclosed sponsorship or personal contacts scraping. Clearly disclose paid creator placements.

## Funnel
Landing → play_start → run_finish → replay → share_click → invited_play_start. Monitor separately: users (privacy-preserving approximate), sessions, completed plays, shares, invite landings. Do not claim perfect de-duplication without proof.

## Risks / mitigations
Slow loading: compressed atlases and preload; high bounce: instant Play; unfair difficulty: tuning; repeated fake runs: backend checks; link previews: OG meta tags plus preview image, network verification.
