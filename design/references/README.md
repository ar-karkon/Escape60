# Escape 60 — Visual reference images

These are concept images, not screenshots of the playable game and not production assets.
Their main purpose is to establish the intended art direction: tropical neon sunset,
voxel 3D, detailed buildings and cars, dramatic composition, vibrant but readable lighting.

01 — main menu
02 — coastal chase
03 — action / roadblock
04 — results

Use these images as a north star; do not turn gameplay into video/background art.
At 390×780, prioritize visible vehicle scale, city silhouette, detailed props, HUD clarity,
and smooth controls. Achieve a playable approximation with custom geometry, texture atlases
and mobile-friendly rendering, rather than trying to reproduce all concept details literally.
